# Notes carrying over

## Set-vs-element rigor (flagged 1B ex. 4)

Jonas flagged confusion between a candidate vector space V (a carrier set)
and its elements. In 1B exercise 4 he described the empty set as though ∅
were itself a vector ("both sides equal the empty set").

Do not let statements like that pass. When a claim conflates a set with its
elements, or treats a vacuous ∀ and a failed ∃ as the same thing, call it out
explicitly rather than reading past it to the intended meaning.
